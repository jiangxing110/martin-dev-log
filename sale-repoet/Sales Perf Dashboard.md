# Sales Commission Dashboard

This is exactly why cash\-basis models create a UI challenge: you are essentially dealing with "time travel\." The rep is sitting in May, generating May revenue, but their May paycheck is tied to cash that arrived in April, which was generated by client activity in March\.

If you just label everything "May," it will cause endless confusion\.

To solve this, the dashboard must completely separate **"What I am generating right now" \(Performance\)** from **"What I am getting paid right now" \(The Wallet\)**\.

Here is exactly how you should structure and label the dashboard when a rep logs in during the month of May\.

## The Dual\-View Dashboard

Split the rep's view into two distinct tabs or side\-by\-side panels\. Never mix the active month's performance with the active month's payout in the same chart\.

### View 1: "My Wallet" \(The Payout View\)

This view tells the rep exactly what is hitting their bank account on the 12th of the current month\. The key is in the labeling—you must explicitly state the source of the funds\.

- **Header:** Upcoming May 12th Payout

- **Total Commission:** $X,XXX

- **How it was calculated:** "Based on total cash collected between April 1 and April 30\."

- **Line Item Breakdown \(Crucial Step\):**

    - *March Processing Invoices \(Paid on April 20\)*

    - *March Conditional Decline Fees \(Paid on April 20\)*

    - *Past\-Due February Invoices \(Finally paid in April\)*

By listing "March Processing" right on the May payout screen, you instantly resolve the timeline confusion\. The rep sees exactly which month's work is funding this paycheck\.

### View 2: "My Performance" \(The Activity View\)

This is your monetization and growth view\. It tracks what the rep is actively doing *today*, even though it won't pay out for another 60 days\.

- **Header:** May Month\-to\-Date \(MTD\) Performance

- **Total Earned Gross Profit \(GP\):** $XX,XXX \(This is the effective revenue \- COGS happening in May\)\.

- **Status Label:** "Currently Unbilled" \(Because May invoices won't go out until June\)\.

- **The "Pipeline" Widget:** Below their May MTD stats, show a "Pending Payouts" pipeline so they know what is coming down the road:

    - **June 12th Pipeline:** $Y,YYY \(Based on April GP, awaiting the May 20th due date\)\.

    - **July 12th Pipeline:** $Z,ZZZ \(Based on their current May GP\)\.

## The Golden Rule for the UI

Never use the phrase "May Commission\." It is too ambiguous\.

Always use **"May 12th Payout"** when referring to the cash hitting their account, and **"May Gross Profit"** when referring to the effective revenue they are generating that month\. Separating the vocabulary prevents 90% of the confusion\.
